# 00 — Project Overview and Charter

## Product definition

**WEBFORGE AI** is an owner-operated AI-assisted website sales platform. It finds suitable businesses, evaluates their public websites, creates tailored redesign concepts and proposals, and manages compliant business outreach. It is not an indiscriminate bulk-email sender, a Maps scraper, a fake-review generator, or an unrestricted autonomous agent.

## Business objective

Help the owner acquire website-development clients by making each approach relevant and tangible: provide specific, evidence-based website observations and a credible independent redesign concept before inviting the business to discuss a project.

## Users

- Owner/operator: configures campaigns, reviews evidence/designs, approves compliance settings, monitors costs and manages replies.
- Future staff: scoped review and reply-handling access.
- Prospect/business: may view a clearly labelled concept and reply/opt out if a contact is legally eligible and provider-permitted.

## In scope

1. Campaign creation by geography, industry and business type.
2. Discovery through approved APIs and permitted independent sources.
3. Data provenance and source-policy enforcement.
4. Deduplication and qualification.
5. Website audit with real browser evidence.
6. Tailored six-screen redesign concept generation.
7. Responsive validation and screenshot capture.
8. Branded PDF proposal and expiring preview URL.
9. Jurisdiction-aware outreach eligibility engine.
10. Email provider adapter, sends, events, replies, opt-outs and suppression.
11. Dashboard, queues, analytics, cost metrics and audit history.
12. Secure 24/7 VPS deployment, backups, alerts and runbooks.

## Out of scope initially

SMS/WhatsApp/social DMs, phone calls, scraping social networks, unauthorized purchased lists, automated negotiation, e-signature/contracts, taking payments, production website launches for prospects, or unrestricted autonomous browsing/shell execution.

## Success measures

- Prospects have verified websites and clear source provenance.
- Audits are evidence-backed and avoid false claims.
- Concepts are coherent, industry-specific and mobile responsive.
- Every proposal opens correctly and is labelled as an independent concept.
- Every message has a traceable eligibility decision and suppression check.
- Owner can pause outbound activity immediately.
- Costs, queue failures and system health are visible.

## Assumptions to validate before production

Owner purchases/controls a domain; creates a professional mailbox; supplies any legally required postal address; obtains provider approval for intended traffic; chooses VPS and region; configures DNS; funds APIs/storage; and obtains qualified legal review for Pakistan and every target jurisdiction.
